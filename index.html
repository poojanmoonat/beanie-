Questions & Care

Everything you need to know before you order.

Need help picking a shade or bulk gifting? Message us directly through checkout.

Every Knot & Loom beanie is knitted in an adaptive unisex one-size fit (54 cm to 61 cm head circumference). The 2×2 rib structure stretches smoothly without pinching and recovers its shape when taken off.

We use a 60% combed merino wool and 40% soft anti-pill acrylic yarn blend. Combed merino brings natural thermal insulation and breathability, while soft acrylic adds structure and prevents scratchiness.

Orders are packed and dispatched within 24 hours. Metro cities deliver in 2–3 business days; rest of India takes 3–5 business days. Shipping is free on orders of ₹999 or more.

We offer 14-day hassle-free returns or color exchanges on unworn beanies with original tags intact. Just message us on WhatsApp with your order details to schedule a pickup.

Hand wash gently in cold water with mild wool detergent. Press excess water into a towel without wringing, and dry flat away from direct heat. Never tumble dry.
